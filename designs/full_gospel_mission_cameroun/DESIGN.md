## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Mission
The design system reflects the enduring heritage and spiritual dynamism of Full Gospel Mission Cameroon (fondée en 1961). It communicates authority, warm communal welcome, vibrant spiritual vitality (the Pentecostal flame), and meticulous modern stewardship. The interface strikes a balance between reverent solemnity and accessible everyday utility across diverse demographics in Cameroon and the diaspora.

### Emotional Response
- **Reverence & Confidence:** Solid typographic foundations evoke stability, pastoral trust, and over six decades of heritage.
- **Spiritual Vitality & Warmth:** Generous warm-white spaces combined with strategic ember-gold and flame-red accents foster an atmosphere of hope, community, and forward movement.
- **Clarity & Reliability:** In transactional spaces—such as tithes, pledges, and event registrations—the UI instills absolute peace of mind through clean layouts, native mobile payment visual patterns, and unambiguous feedback.

### Design Aesthetic
The aesthetic is **Warm Modern Minimalist with African Contextual Practicality**:
- Clean surface compositions built over a tactile, warm white canvas (`#F7F6F4`) rather than harsh sterile whites or clinical grays.
- Humanist typography paired with structured tabular systems for church metrics and FCFA financial offerings.
- Generous touch targets designed for multi-generational smartphone usage across varied connection qualities in Central Africa.
- Seamless bilingual architecture ensuring equal dignity and legibility in both French and English.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette translates the spiritual symbolism of the Pentecostal flame into functional UI tokens while meeting strict WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility ratios against light surfaces.

### Primary Spectrum: Flame Red
- **Primary Main (`#C8102E`):** Sacred flame; reserved for key brand identifiers, primary calls to action (giving, joining, live stream access), and critical highlighted states.
- **Primary Hover/Pressed (`#A30D25`):** High-contrast interactive state.
- **Primary Soft / Badge Tint (`#FDECEF`):** Subtle background for status indicators, active category pills, and pastoral announcements.

### Secondary Spectrum: Ember Gold
- **Secondary Main (`#E8A33D`):** Symbolizes the divine, stewardship milestones, anniversary highlights (1961–present), key numeric impacts, and sacred ceremonial badges.

### Neutral Foundation
- **Canvas / App Surface (`#F7F6F4`):** Warm-bone white surface reducing ocular fatigue and imbuing physical warmth.
- **Container / Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure elevated white for distinct content modules and reading planes.
- **Border Subtle (`#E7E4E0`):** Soft, low-contrast bounding lines for cards, list dividers, and inputs.
- **Text Primary (`#0B0B0F`):** Deep ink-black delivering crisp readability.
- **Text Charcoal (`#1C1C22`):** Grounded body and headings in nested components.
- **Text Secondary / Muted (`#5B5B66`):** Secondary metadata, timestamps, verse references, and placeholder text.

### Contextual & Mobile Payment Tokens
- **MTN MoMo & Success (`#1F9D55`):** Completed offerings, payment verifications, affirmative actions.
- **Orange Money (`#FF7900`):** Official payment method indicator, pending financial alerts.
- **Info & Scripture Reference (`#2F6FDE`):** Pastoral announcements, study links, auxiliary notifications.
- **Warning (`#D98A0B`):** System notifications, grace periods, pledge reminders.
- **Error (`#B3261E`):** Failed transactions, validation errors, urgent cancellation notices.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Headings:** Set in *Plus Jakarta Sans*. Its geometric warmth and contemporary open apertures convey vibrancy without sacrificing pastoral gravity.
- **Body & Functional Text:** Set in *Inter*. Selected for neutral, high-legibility rendering across varied resolutions, low-end mobile devices, and screen sizes.

### Numeric & Currency Styling
All amounts expressed in FCFA (XAF) and metric data must apply OpenType tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum"`). This prevents horizontal shifting during calculations and aligns financial statements precisely across ledgers and mobile payment checkouts.

### Bilingual Layout Guidance
- English text typically requires 15% to 20% less horizontal real estate than French. All button containers, tabs, and navigation anchors must use flexible box models with ellipsis prevention or deliberate two-line wrap handling.
- Avoid all-caps transformations on long French strings to maintain immediate legibility and diacritic integrity (accents on É, À, È).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The system uses an adaptive fluid-grid framework tuned for responsive mobile-first touch interfaces:
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) outer margin, 16px gutter. Compact vertical stacking.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin, 24px gutter. Dual-pane flows for sermon libraries and administrative lists.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, capped at a maximum content width of 1280px with centered auto-margins, 32px (`2rem`) gutters.

### Touch Target Standard
In adherence to accessibility norms and single-handed mobile usage during church services, all touchable UI elements (buttons, payment toggles, audio playback bars, prayer submitters) must enforce a strict **minimum bounding box of 44px × 44px**, with 48px preferred for primary actions.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon an 8-point base grid:
- `space-xs` (4px): Dense element internal pad (pills, micro badges).
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-label spacing, compact form field groupings.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard element padding, card internal margins, horizontal list gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Sectional separation within cards, card grids.
- `space-xl` (40px): Major page division, between header hero and content feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Atmosphere
Elevation relies on smooth, warm-tinted ambient shadows rather than stark neutral drops, creating a soft physical presence on the `#F7F6F4` background canvas:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Substrate):** Surface background `#F7F6F4`. No shadow. Uses soft border `#E7E4E0` when segregation is required.
- **Level 1 (Card / Rest):** Raised elements on pure white `#FFFFFF`. 
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 11, 15, 0.04), 0 4px 16px 0 rgba(11, 11, 15, 0.03);`
  Border: `1px solid #E7E4E0`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Elements):** Active cards, floating audio sermon pill, payment option selected.
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.06), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 11, 15, 0.06);`
- **Level 3 (Modal / Bottom Sheet / Flyout):** Full transactional sheets (MoMo/Orange PIN confirmations, language selectors).
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 11, 15, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(11, 11, 15, 0.04);`

### Border Accents
No harsh black borders. Micro-borders (`1px solid #E7E4E0`) are placed systematically on card containers to maintain structural legibility even on low-brightness outdoor screen conditions.

## Shapes

### Roundedness Hierarchy
The structural identity uses a unified 16px (`1rem`) standard curvature across standard cards and major content nodes, establishing an inviting, contemporary visual signature:

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** All content modules, giving cards, announcements, sermon thumbnails, and input containers.
- **Pills / Interactive Tokens (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status badges, tag filters, live stream indicators, primary buttons, and quick-amount selectors (e.g., `1 000 FCFA`, `5 000 FCFA`).
- **Inner Micro Elements (`rounded` / 8px):** Checkboxes, inner icon wrappers, nested alert callouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Flame Red):** Background `#C8102E`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, height 48px min, radius 9999px (full pill) or 16px (in inline card actions). Active state `#A30D25`. Shadow level 1.
- **Secondary / Stewardship Action (Ember Gold):** Background `#E8A33D`, text `#0B0B0F`, font-weight 600. Used for special projects, church planting drives, or conference registrations.
- **Neutral Outlined:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #E7E4E0`, text `#0B0B0F`, hover background `#FFFFFF`. Height 48px.
- **Touch Affirmation:** Tactile scale response `transform: scale(0.98)` on touch-down.

### Payment & Offering Selectors (MTN MoMo & Orange Money)
- **Method Toggle Cards:** White background cards (`rounded-lg` 16px) featuring official brand badges.
  - Selected MTN state: 2px border `#1F9D55`, light tint surface `#E8F6EE`.
  - Selected Orange Money state: 2px border `#FF7900`, light tint surface `#FFF4EB`.
- **Pre-set Offering Chips:** Full-pill buttons displaying pre-set amounts (`1 000`, `2 500`, `5 000`, `10 000 FCFA`) with tabular figures. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` with `#E7E4E0` border. Active: `#C8102E` with white text.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Content Cards:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E4E0`, radius 16px, padding 16px (mobile) to 24px (desktop).
- **Featured Sermon / Live Stream Card:** High-contrast dark charcoal surface `#1C1C22`, text `#FFFFFF`, primary flame accent tag, streaming indicator with animated pulse badge.

### Form Inputs & Financial Entry
- **Field Height:** 48px minimum height.
- **Border & Background:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E7E4E0`, radius 12px or 16px, text `#0B0B0F`, placeholder `#5B5B66`.
- **Focus State:** 2px border `#C8102E` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px #FDECEF`.
- **Currency Field Adornment:** Fixed trailing label `"FCFA"` set in medium weight tabular typography.

### Chips & Filters
- **Department & Ministry Tags:** (Jeunesse, Dames, Hommes, Chorale / Youth, Women, Men, Choir). Height 36px, radius 9999px. Unselected: background `#FFFFFF`, text `#5B5B66`, border `#E7E4E0`. Selected: background `#FDECEF`, text `#C8102E`, border `#C8102E`.

### Language Switcher (FR / EN)
- Floating or header-docked segment control: Pill container (`#FFFFFF` with `#E7E4E0` border), containing two 36px circular or rounded sliders. The active language is emphasized with `#0B0B0F` background and `#FFFFFF` text.

### Audio / Sermon Persistent Player
- Docked bottom sheet or floating pill bar resting above navigation: Pure white or deep charcoal container with Level 3 elevation, play/pause controls (min 44px hit-box), progress track, and scripture passage reference.
